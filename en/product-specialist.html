A specialist is not a chat with a prompt. A chat has no memory: close the window and it knows nothing about you again. A specialist has a memory of its own, it builds up day by day and never resets, so tomorrow it remembers what was decided yesterday, and why it was decided that way.

One specialist — one area and one memory. It answers for its direction the way a person in that job would, and its work can be checked.

Where it works

Inside the companyruns a department: content, automation, bookkeeping, legal — under the second brain
Out fronttalks to customers: takes orders, answers questions, books people in

The only difference is which way it faces. It is built the same either way — its own memory, its own area, its own rules of work.

What it is given to do

  • Orderstakes the order, checks the details, writes down the address and the time. The customer is not waiting for a free pair of hands
  • Shiftsputs people on shifts and watches that no gaps are left in the schedule
  • Hiringtalks to candidates, asks the questions that matter and brings the owner an answer, not a stack of applications
  • A departmentinside the company it runs a whole direction: promotion, automation, finances — under the second brain
