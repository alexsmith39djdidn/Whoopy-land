What it is

An AI specialist is an employee responsible for one area. It doesn't suggest what to do or explain how to pay for an order: it works out the delivery, issues the invoice, checks the payment and logs the order. On its own, with no person in the middle.

How it's built

  • Workspulls its own tools by itself: works out delivery, issues the invoice, checks the payment, logs the order, notifies the owner
  • Learnsreviews its own work and adjusts its rules from it. Pudra's rule of "don't message anyone who's complained about spam" was one the bot proposed itself — we looked it over and kept it. It doesn't change rules quietly: it shows them to you first
  • Remembers what it doesremembers what was decided yesterday, and why it was decided that way. Memory builds up day by day and never resets, so the area doesn't start over every morning
One specialist — one area, one memory, its own hands. It answers for its area the same way a person in that job would.

Where it works

Inside the companyruns a department: content, automation, bookkeeping, legal — under the second brain
Out fronttalks to customers: takes orders, answers questions, sees things through to payment

The only difference is which way it's facing. It's built the same either way — its own memory, its own area, its own rules of work and its own set of tools.

What it's given to do

  • Orderstakes the order, checks the details, works out delivery and takes payment. The customer isn't waiting for a free pair of hands
  • Shiftsputs people on shifts and makes sure no gaps are left in the schedule
  • Hiringtalks to candidates, asks the questions that matter and brings the owner a result — not a stack of applications
  • A departmentinside the company it runs a whole area: promotion, automation, finance — under the second brain

What it doesn't do

  • Doesn't make things upif the answer isn't in what it's been given, it doesn't invent one — it hands the conversation to a person, with the whole exchange attached
  • Doesn't decide for youprices, discounts, terms and rules are yours to set. It works within them and only changes them on your word
  • Doesn't go in overnightit's built for the task: tools, rules, memory, testing. A finished specialist is weeks of work, not an on switch
