A person is strong at one thing. If you are a master of your craft, then that is what you are — not an accountant, not a marketer, not a head of planning. But the running of the company still falls to you, and it takes the strength that could have gone into the work itself.

Stay strong at what you are strongest at — the second brain takes everything else. It is not an assistant and not a bot. It is a company run from the inside: strategy, planning, marketing, finances, the logic of decisions.

What it takes over

Planningwhat the company is doing this week, and why this and not something else
Financeswhere the money goes, what pays off, where the hole is
Marketing and promotionwhat the company says to the outside
Tasks and follow-throughwho is doing what, and what of it is done
Memoryeverything the company has learned about itself sits in one place and never resets

Inside, the brain is built out of departments. Each department has its own area, its own memory and its own AI specialist — or a person, whom the brain works with on the same terms.

What it’s made of

  • Departmentsas many as your company needs: promotion, finances, automation, legal. Each with its own area of responsibility and its own rules of work
  • Specialistsa department is run by an AI specialist — or by a person, and then the brain works with the human exactly as it works with the AI
  • Memorydecisions, numbers, agreements and reasons build up day by day. The company stops forgetting why something was done that way six months ago
  • Ongoing supportthe brain does not stay the way it was installed: every month new departments, rules and people appear in it

What its week looks like

Writing a smart plan is one thing; carrying it through the week is another. Everyone has plans and task lists, and for almost everyone they go dead by Wednesday. A brain is not a document. It is work that keeps moving on its own.

  • The week’s planwhat the company is doing on these days, and why this and not whatever came to hand first
  • Taskscut so that each one is done in a single sitting, and each says in advance how we will know it is finished
  • Projectsrun from the first step to the mark “done”, with a report at every stage — the work is visible instead of having to be asked for
  • Reviewsa regular audit: what worked, what did not, what changes next week. A mistake turns into a rule instead of repeating itself
  • Talking to peoplethe brain writes to your staff itself — sets a task, takes the work in, asks a question when something does not add up

How it comes into a company

Nobody will hand control over to a machine on day one, and they are right not to. So the brain takes the wheel in steps, proving itself at each one:

1 · AdviserThe brain sees the whole company and proposes decisions. You still decide.
2 · DepartmentsThe brain takes whole directions: planning, finances, promotion.
3 · Full internal controlThe brain runs the company from the inside, and you go back to what you are best at.

For the first two or three months we run the brain and grow it. After that it works on its own.
